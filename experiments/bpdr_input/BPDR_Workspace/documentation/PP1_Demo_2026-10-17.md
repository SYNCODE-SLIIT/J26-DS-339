# PP1 demonstration: validation path and rule-based correction - 17 October 2026

**What the demo shows:** the input component taking a recording end to end. It keeps the original, runs technical checks, decides a hand-off status, and tries the proposal's conservative rule-based pitch-centre correction (Table 2, a non-learning reference condition). Every edit goes through permission limits, Praat rendering, re-measurement and rollback. The learned BPDR transformer is shown as a diagnosed experiment, not as a working repair.

```powershell
.\Run-Demo.ps1 -Source <recording.wav or a demo input folder>
.\Run-Demo.ps1 -Source <...> -HandOffPrepared   # verified candidates become ACCEPT_PREPARED packages
```

## Rule

Implemented in `src/bpdr_input/rule_correction.py`, following proposal section 3.3:
- **Grid:** the singer's own tuning offset (circular mean of fine YIN pitch), then all twelve semitones. No key or scale is assumed.
- **Notes:** reliable frames are those where YIN and PESTO agree within 50 cents. A note ends when pitch leaves its last 0.5 s level by more than 50 cents.
- **Correction:** one constant correction per stable held note, applied by scaling Praat's measured pitch tier, so vibrato and contour are kept.
- **Fixed thresholds:**
  - correct only deviations of 10-30 cents; larger ones are withheld, never clipped;
  - withhold notes whose detrended spread or drift exceeds 12 cents (vibrato or slide);
  - notes must be at least 0.86 s long (the renderer's minimum).
- **Existing path, unchanged:**
  - `repair_controller.plan_fixture`: at most 30 cents, at least 0.86 s of support, at most 25% of the recording affected;
  - `framewise_renderer.render_candidate`: Praat rendering;
  - `framewise_audit.audit_curve`: re-measurement;
  - `transact_fixture`: rollback.

The rule is conservative, with low coverage. It mostly withholds, and it edits about one original in six on Jamendo. It does nothing on Vocadito, because its held notes are shorter than the 0.86-s support minimum. These are engineering results on development data, not musical acceptance or a calibrated policy.

## The learned BPDR transformer: controlled proof of concept

Explainer page: `runs/demo/bpdr_explainer/bpdr_explainer.html` (linked from the demo index). Code: `controlled_study.py` (data, training), `bpdr_showcase.py` (page and end-to-end example).

**Data.** Constructed hummed phrases from 22 synthetic voices: 16 for training and 6 held out. The voices differ in range, tuning offset, timbre and vibrato. Each phrase is an accepted reference with a local ±10/20/30-cent shift inside a held note, made by the component's own intervention renderer (`render_local`, 80-ms ramps), plus false split/merge/jitter boundary views. Features come from the same extraction path as real audio (PESTO, log-mel, energy, onset, fine YIN pitch, timing-only boundary track). That gives 384 training and 72 held-out records.

**Training.** The three proposal models were trained with matched initialization, optimizer, schedule and budget (1,500 updates, batch 8). The objective is a squared-error L_A + 0.1 L_S, and BPDR adds L_B + L_D after a 100-update warm-up. The input is phrase-relative fine pitch.

**Held-out synthetic voices, three seeds** (correction error in cents, no correction 19.3; response gain in brackets):

| Model | Seed 339 | Seed 340 | Seed 341 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Augmentation-only (71) | 7.16 (1.04) | 17.26 (0.00) | 6.32 (0.99) |
| Frame-only (70) | 5.40 (1.01) | 15.36 (0.00) | 6.58 (0.95) |
| BPDR (71) | 4.49 (0.92) | 18.58 (0.00) | 5.76 (1.00) |

Change to unshifted originals is 0.04-0.19 cents in the seeds that learn. Boundary sensitivity is 0.01-0.05 cents for the boundary-aware models.

**End-to-end example (seed 339).** The fixed rule picks the first held-out record with a 20-cent shift and a false split.
- **Detuned audio.** The BPDR prediction went through permission (runtime voicing only), Praat rendering, re-measurement and rollback. The audit passed (`VERIFIED_FIXTURE_CANDIDATE`). On the detuned note the error moved from -19.8 to -3.3 cents against the original.
- **Unshifted original, same chain.** Outcome `IDENTITY`: no edit proposed, and the largest prediction on voiced frames was 0.10 cents.
- **Demo-only span rule.** The frame curve becomes one edit by keeping the largest contiguous voiced span asking for at least 3 cents. This rule is not part of the proposal.

**Reading.**
- **When training succeeds** (seeds 339 and 341), all three transformers learn the counter-detuning response on unseen voices and leave original singing essentially unchanged. That is the mechanism the proposal describes, working in the implemented code.
- **Seed 340** collapsed to zero output for all three models. This is the same failure as on real data, so training is sensitive to initialization and sampling order.
- **BPDR versus augmentation-only.** They are not separated across seeds.
- **Boundary invariance.** The hypothesised advantage is not shown, because boundary sensitivity is tiny for all models.
- **Design confound.** In every constructed phrase the detuned note is also the longest note.
- **Real singing.** The transformer still does not transfer to new singers. Two defects were found and fixed on the way: the 33-cent pitch resolution and the mean-absolute anchor.

## Reproduce offline

`runs/` is not tracked by git. Keep a copy of `runs/demo/` and `runs/controlled_study/` for the presentation, and rebuild them with the commands below if needed. Run all of them from `experiments/bpdr_input`.

```powershell
# CPU environment (.venv): rule-based demo
$env:PYTHONPATH='src'; .venv\Scripts\python.exe -m bpdr_input.rule_evaluation
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m bpdr_input.demo build-set --evaluation-run runs\rule_evaluation\<id>
.\Run-Demo.ps1 -Source runs\demo_inputs\<id>

# CPU environment: controlled data (audio + features, ~10 min)
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m bpdr_input.controlled_study build

# Intel Arc XPU environment: three matched models (~15 min per seed); seeds 340 and 341 are replicates
checkpoints\melody-extraction\.venv-xpu\Scripts\python.exe -m bpdr_input.controlled_study train
checkpoints\melody-extraction\.venv-xpu\Scripts\python.exe -m bpdr_input.controlled_study train 340
checkpoints\melody-extraction\.venv-xpu\Scripts\python.exe -m bpdr_input.controlled_study train 341

# CPU environment: explainer page and the end-to-end example
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m bpdr_input.bpdr_showcase runs\demo\bpdr_explainer
```

## Limitations

- Thresholds are fixed engineering values, without listening tests or calibration.
- YIN both decides the correction and checks it, with PESTO as the second tracker.
- The constructed hums are controls, not voices.
- Participant (novice) recordings still need ethics approval.
- No `ACCEPT_PREPARED` package is produced unless `-HandOffPrepared` is set.
- E/F open, D/J4 deferred, research-ready examples zero.
