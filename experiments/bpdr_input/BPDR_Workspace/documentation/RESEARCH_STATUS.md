# Research status

**Updated: 28 October 2026.** Use the [master plan](BPDR_Master_Plan.md), [tracker](BPDR_Implementation_Tracker.md) and [planning review](Planning_Review_2026-10-27.md). Earlier dated records retain their outcomes; old next-step text is not the current task order.

The proposal asks whether BPDR improves local pitch repair and subsequent melody extraction under segmentation errors without increasing harmful alteration at matched acceptance coverage. The methodological question is the benefit of boundary-invariance and counter-detuning objectives with preservation anchors, relative to matched augmentation-only and frame-only controls. Reliable real-voice methodological superiority remains **unestablished**.

The current guarded learned demo uses experimental ExtraTrees: 96 trees / 31 own-audio context features, selected as a diagnostic fallback after declared full criteria failed. Its earlier delivered-WAV pilot had four scoreable interventions out of sixteen attempts from one scoreable singer, two improvements and four preserved originals. Excluding boundary metadata gives invariance by construction, not learned disentanglement.

The new context Transformer completed a separate development experiment. On ten scoreable calibration interventions out of sixteen attempts, from two singers / three scoreable original recordings, its BPDR-condition raw MAE is **5.839 cents** versus **10.031** for matched ExtraTrees. Augmentation-only is **5.644** and frame-only **5.652**; this seed does not show a BPDR-objective advantage. Features, architecture and squared response terms are a declared engineering redesign, not an exact repeat of the proposal's 71-feature / MAE-response study.

Better raw predictions have not become useful repairs in the recorded permission replay: seven cases withheld, two identity and one permitted edit outside the scored shifted region; permitted MAE remains **25.833 cents**. No WAV was rendered in that replay. The planning diagnosis finds zero scope-eligible scored frames in seven of ten cases. Existing Praat rendering, decoded-WAV audits and rollback are implemented helpers; usable integration of the new candidate remains pending.

The immediate engineering target is verified repair of stable held-voice regions, followed by **Selective Vocal Repair with Protected Regions** in the BPDR app only. Region-level protection, individual Accept/Keep and final composition are **planned**, not completed. They support the workflow but do not replace the research hypothesis.

No objective passes every held-out singer's prediction gate. The combined candidate is a diagnostic selection, not an overall accuracy win or a newly activated demo model.
